# Product Requirement Document (PRD) - BioFi-Nexus

## 1. Executive Summary
BioFi-Nexus is a cutting-edge platform designed to bridge the gap between biological data and financial technology (BioFi). By leveraging advanced data analytics, blockchain, and secure data handling, BioFi-Nexus enables researchers, healthcare providers, and financial institutions to collaborate, trade data-backed assets, and fund biological research through innovative financial instruments.

## 2. Project Goals
- **Data Integration**: Seamlessly ingest and process diverse biological data types, including genomic, proteomic, and clinical data.
- **Financial Innovation**: Create a robust marketplace for bio-data assets and provide novel research funding mechanisms.
- **Security & Privacy**: Ensure the highest standards of data protection and regulatory compliance (HIPAA, GDPR).
- **Interoperability**: Connect with existing biological databases, EHR systems, and financial platforms.

## 3. Target Audience
- **Biological Researchers & Institutions**: Looking for funding and data sharing opportunities.
- **Biotech Startups**: Needing access to data and capital.
- **Financial Investors & Hedge Funds**: Seeking exposure to the biotech sector through data-backed assets.
- **Healthcare Providers**: Wanting to monetize data securely or access advanced research.

## 4. Key Features (Phase 1)
- **Bio-Data Vault**: A secure, encrypted repository for storing and managing large-scale biological datasets.
- **Asset Tokenization Engine**: A toolset to convert research findings, datasets, or IP into tradable digital assets (tokens).
- **Bio-Marketplace**: A decentralized platform to list, discover, and trade bio-financial instruments and data assets.
- **Analytics Dashboard**: Comprehensive tools for real-time insights into data value, research milestones, and market trends.
- **Compliance Module**: Automated checks and balances to ensure data handling stays within legal and ethical boundaries.

## 5. Technical Stack
- **Frontend**: Next.js (React), Tailwind CSS, Lucide React (icons).
- **Backend**: Node.js, TypeScript, Express.
- **Database**: Supabase (PostgreSQL) for structured data and metadata.
- **Storage**: Secure cloud storage (AWS S3 or similar) with encryption.
- **Infrastructure**: Render for hosting and deployment.
- **Authentication**: Supabase Auth for secure user management.

## 6. Roadmap
- **Q1 (MVP)**: Core platform development, Bio-Data Vault, and basic User Management.
- **Q2**: Marketplace launch and initial asset tokenization tools.
- **Q3**: Integration with external biological databases and expansion of analytics features.
- **Q4**: Global rollout, regulatory certification, and advanced financial modeling tools.
